Password Strength Checker

How Strong Is Your Password?

Type any password below to see how strong it is, what makes it weak or strong, and how to improve it. Nothing you type here is saved, sent, or stored anywhere — this runs entirely in your browser.

  • ○ At least 6 characters
  • ○ 10 or more characters
  • ○ Uppercase letter (A–Z)
  • ○ Lowercase letter (a–z)
  • ○ Number (0–9)
  • ○ Special character (!@#$…)

💡 A better approach: Passphrases

Instead of a hard-to-remember jumble, try stringing four random words together. They're longer, stronger, and much easier to remember. Click any example below to test it above.

Privacy note

This tool runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is saved, stored, or sent anywhere. Use a real password or make one up — either way, it stays on your screen only.

Next step: a password manager

Once you have strong passwords, the challenge is remembering them. A password manager (like Bitwarden — free) stores all your passwords securely and fills them in for you. You only need to remember one main password.
