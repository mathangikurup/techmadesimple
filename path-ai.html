Understanding AI — Without the Hype

Artificial intelligence is now part of everyday life — in your phone, your email, your search results, and tools like ChatGPT. This path explains what AI is, how to use it safely, and how to stay smart about it — no technical background needed.

Why this matters right now

AI tools are changing faster than any technology in recent memory. You don't need to be an expert — but understanding the basics protects you from misinformation, scams, and accidental privacy mistakes. This path is updated regularly as the technology evolves.
