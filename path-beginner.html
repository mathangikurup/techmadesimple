Beginner Path

I've Never Used a Computer

Start here — no experience needed. These 6 short lessons take you from "what is a computer?" all the way to getting online and writing your first email. Go at your own pace, and revisit any lesson as many times as you like.

✓ Turn a computer on and off safely ✓ Use a mouse and keyboard with confidence ✓ Open a web browser and search online ✓ Send and receive your first email
01

What Is a Computer?

Learn the different parts of a computer — screen, keyboard, mouse, and more — and what each one does. Covers desktops, laptops, and tablets.

⏱ 5 min All Ages
→
02

Using a Mouse & Touchpad

How to click, double-click, scroll, and right-click. If you're on a laptop, learn how to use the touchpad instead of a mouse.

⏱ 7 min All Ages
→
03

Typing Basics

Find the letters, numbers, and important keys like Backspace, Enter, and Shift. Practice typing a few sentences at your own pace.

⏱ 8 min All Ages
→
04

Turning On, Off & Restarting Safely

The right way to start and shut down a computer — and why it matters. Learn what to do if a computer freezes or behaves unexpectedly.

⏱ 5 min All Ages
→
05

Opening a Browser & Searching Online

What a web browser is (Chrome, Firefox, Safari), how to type a web address, and how to search for anything using Google or another search engine.

⏱ 8 min All Ages
→
06

Connecting to Wi-Fi & Your First Email

How to connect your device to Wi-Fi at home or in a library. Then: create a free email account and send your very first message.

⏱ 10 min All Ages
→

You can't break anything

Computers are designed to be explored. If something doesn't work, you can always restart and try again. Ask a family member, friend, or librarian to sit with you — learning together is much easier.
